Exact shot targets, framing and intervals are in shot-plan.json. Foreground lamps, stoops and bicycles are dimensional and occlude correctly. Character drawings remain 2D cutouts, deliberately retaining the illustrated style; this is a 2.5D cartoon, not volumetric human anatomy.
