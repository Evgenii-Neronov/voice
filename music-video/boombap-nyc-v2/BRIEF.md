# NYC boombap v2: production brief

Track: 07-minimax-trio-boombap-01.mp3, original generated voice, 253.283265 seconds. Preserve v1.

Premise: a street performance draws a passing listener into a brief shared moment before he continues his evening walk.

Mandatory: 2560x1440/24 fps; existing lead identity/yellow jacket; yellow subtitles raised into player-safe region; independent listening companions on the road; natural lowered arms; actual backwards tracking camera and opening walk; occasional shallow knee bends; audio-derived visemes; sidewalk boombox; bicycle by building; reference-inspired male passerby, nominal body height 183 cm, fedora/trenchcoat/tie; edge-position ADV graffiti on pale wall; NY on cap. Only ADV, NY and subtitles are authorized lettering.

0-6.8s: medium tracking shot, lead walks toward camera, camera retreats on road. Companions behind.
6.8-35s: medium/oblique/close performance coverage, independent listeners.
35-47s: wider shot introduces passerby walking along sidewalk.
47-64s: passerby pauses and listens, rap remains visual focus.
64-79s: passerby continues and leaves; camera shows the street group in context.
79-241s: alternating medium, oblique, close, and occasional wide shots tied to phrase boundaries.
241-253s: camera withdraws to reveal the group and neighborhood.

Technical corrections: motion FBX rest orientations differ from avatar rest orientations. Retarget local anatomical rotations rather than directly assigning animation curves. Mouth is driven by locally inferred acoustic phoneme peaks, not audio loudness alone. CTC timings and viseme mapping are approximate and need visual review, not a claim of performance capture.

The reference image only supplies front portrait/wardrobe cues. Passerby is a 3D approximation, not a reconstructed exact likeness. Existing Rocketbox lead identity is preserved; subdivision and material refinement do not turn the source into a newly scanned human.

Sources: Microsoft Rocketbox MIT characters/motion, Poly Haven CC0 surfaces and HDRI, locally constructed props. facebook/wav2vec2-xlsr-53-espeak-cv-ft downloaded into I:/VOICE/voice/models/lipsync/wav2vec2-espeak (weights excluded from git). NVIDIA Audio2Face SDK evaluated as an alternative but not used: requires additional CUDA/TensorRT/build setup.
